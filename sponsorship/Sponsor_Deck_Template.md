2) Silver — Mid-Tier

3) Gold — Premium

4) Custom Campaigns / Exclusive Sponsorship

## Sample Rate Card (example calculations)
- If you estimate 10,000 monthly impressions on the player page and a banner CPM of $5, price = 10,000/1000 * $5 = $50/month for the banner.
- Audio CPMs are higher — a $20 CPM for a 30s host-read with 1,000 listens = $20 * 1 = $20 (scale accordingly).
